The four principles behind every structure

No riba
Return comes from trade, leasing or shared enterprise, never from lending money for a fixed surplus.
Real asset or real enterprise
Each structure is tied to a tangible asset, a service or a business venture.
Clarity (no excessive gharar)
Price, term, ownership and obligations are known and written before signing.
Fair risk
Whoever is entitled to profit carries a matching share of risk.
